Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about ThreatX, quantum-safe messaging, and the BB84 behind it.

ThreatX is a secure quantum-safe messaging platform that combines Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) with AES-GCM encryption to ensure end-to-end privacy.
QKD uses quantum mechanics (such as the BB84 protocol) to securely exchange cryptographic keys. Any attempt to eavesdrop alters the quantum states, alerting the users.
While no system is 100% unbreakable, combining QKD with AES-GCM provides strong protection against both classical and quantum computer attacks.
Real-world QKD requires quantum hardware. However, ThreatX offers a simulation-based demo that works without special devices.

ThreatX primarily uses AES-256-GCM for message encryption and integrates keys exchanged securely using Quantum Key Distribution.

ThreatX is designed for researchers, students, cybersecurity enthusiasts, and organizations interested in quantum-safe communication.
With QKD, any attempt to eavesdrop is detectable. Messages are also encrypted with AES-GCM, making unauthorized access extremely difficult.
ThreatX supports the BB84 quantum key distribution protocol and uses standard AES-GCM for secure message encryption and authentication.
Quantum key generation can be slower than traditional methods, but once established, AES-GCM ensures fast and efficient message encryption/decryption.
